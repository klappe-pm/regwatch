# Pricing Copy

Managed text for the pricing copy surface. Edit a value after its key, then rebuild the web app.

- `your-business`: Your business.
- `your-watchlist`: Your watchlist.
- `start-with-the-workflow-choose-your-subscription`: Start with the workflow. Choose your subscription when paid membership launches.
- `bev-watch-membership`: Bev Watch Membership
- `a-focused-home-for-your-beverage-portfolio`: A focused home for your beverage portfolio and regulatory reviews.
- `public-preview`: Public preview
- `no-charge`: No charge
- `subscription-pricing-has-not-been-published-no`: Subscription pricing has not been published. No card is collected, and exploring the preview does not start a subscription.
- `try-the-workspace`: Try the workspace
- `live-monitoring-and-team-accounts-are-not`: Live monitoring and team accounts are not active.
- `a-few-useful-details`: A Few Useful Details.
- `what-can-i-use-today`: What can I use today?
- `you-can-set-up-a-portfolio-choose`: You can set up a portfolio, choose markets and sources, explore synthetic regulatory updates, save review decisions, and export a sample briefing. Your workspace stays in this browser.
- `does-this-preview-monitor-official-sources`: Does this preview monitor official sources?
- `no-the-public-website-demonstrates-the-review`: No. The public website demonstrates the review workflow with clearly labeled sample updates. The Regwatch engine supports source checking locally. Hosted monitoring needs a production backend before launch.
- `will-i-be-charged-automatically`: Will I be charged automatically?
- `no-the-preview-has-no-payment-collection`: No. The preview has no payment collection, recurring billing, or paid entitlements. Future subscriptions will require an explicit checkout.
- `does-bev-watch-give-legal-advice`: Does Bev Watch give legal advice?
- `no-regulatory-information-and-potential-impacts-require`: No. Regulatory information and potential impacts require verification against primary sources and review by a qualified person.
