# documents

The Documents page of the local application, a premium feature: documents built from a template that follows the design specification.

## `header`

- `title`: Documents
- `description`: Documents built from a template that follows your design specification: headings, colours, typeface and logo, set once in Settings.
- `feature`: Documents

## `controls`

- `document`: Document
- `detail`: Detail
- `generate`: Generate Summary
- `generating`: Generating

## `kinds`

- `briefing`: Briefing
- `digest`: Register Digest
- `report`: Change and Due Report

## `notes`

- `pdf`: To save a PDF, download the HTML, open it in your browser and print it. The page size comes from your design.
- `writing`: Writing the summary. This can take a minute.
- `failed`: The summary could not be generated.
- `preview`: Document preview
