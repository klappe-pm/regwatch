# obligations

The Obligations page of the local application: the register, read only, with the products each obligation may affect.

## `header`

- `title`: Obligations
- `description`: Your register, read only, with the products each obligation may affect. Edit the register files to change it.

## `filters`

- `filter`: Filter
- `filter-placeholder`: Title, citation or id
- `jurisdiction`: Jurisdiction
- `all-jurisdictions`: All jurisdictions

## `table`

- `label`: Obligations
- `empty`: No obligation matches these filters.
- `obligation`: Obligation
- `jurisdiction`: Jurisdiction
- `status`: Status
- `effective`: Effective
- `impacts`: Impacts

## `detail`

- `id`: Id
- `instrument`: Instrument
- `citation`: Citation
- `categories`: Categories
- `no-categories`: None
- `impacts`: Impacts on Your Products
- `no-impacts`: None of your products is affected.
