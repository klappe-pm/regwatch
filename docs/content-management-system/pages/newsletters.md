# newsletters

The Newsletters page of the local application, a premium feature: a send-ready newsletter assembled from a period's changes.

## `header`

- `title`: Newsletters
- `description`: A send-ready newsletter assembled from the period's changes, as HTML email with a plain text part. Bev Watch does not send email; you review it and send it from your own mail tool.
- `feature`: Newsletters

## `controls`

- `period`: Period
- `generate`: Generate
- `generating`: Generating
- `copy-html`: Copy HTML
- `copy-text`: Copy Text
- `email`: Email (.eml)

## `periods`

- `7`: Last 7 days
- `14`: Last 14 days
- `30`: Last 30 days
- `90`: Last 90 days

## `notes`

- `fixed`: The opening and summaries are fixed sentences built from your data. Turn on generated copy in Settings to have a language model write them.
- `writing`: Writing the opening and summaries. This can take a minute.
- `failed`: The copy could not be generated.
- `copy-failed`: The {what} could not be copied; download it instead.
- `preview`: Newsletter preview
