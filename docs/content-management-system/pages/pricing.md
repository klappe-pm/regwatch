## `plan`

- `title`: Bev Watch Membership

- `status`: Public preview

## `faq`

- `title`: A Few Useful Details.
- `1-question`: What can I use today?
- `1-answer`: You can set up a portfolio, choose markets and sources, explore synthetic regulatory updates, save review decisions, and export a sample briefing. Your workspace stays in this browser.
- `2-question`: Does this preview monitor official sources?
- `2-answer`: No. The public website demonstrates the review workflow with clearly labeled sample updates. The Regwatch engine supports source checking locally. Hosted monitoring needs a production backend before launch.
- `3-question`: Will I be charged automatically?
- `3-answer`: No. The preview has no payment collection, recurring billing, or paid entitlements. Future subscriptions will require an explicit checkout.
- `4-question`: Does Bev Watch give legal advice?
- `4-answer`: No. Regulatory information and potential impacts require verification against primary sources and review by a qualified person.
