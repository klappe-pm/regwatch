# onboarding

The five-step setup of the public preview: business, markets, sources, briefing preference, and a summary before the workspace opens.

## `meta`

- `title`: Set Up Your Watchlist

## `frame`

- `explore`: Explore first
- `aside-1`: A watchlist
- `aside-2`: that knows
- `aside-emphasis`: your business.
- `progress`: Setup progress
- `saved-note`: Your choices are saved in this browser as you continue. No account or payment is created.
- `step-count`: Step {step} of {total}

## `steps`

- `1-name`: Your business
- `1-title`: What do you make?
- `1-intro`: Start with the business and beverage categories you want to keep in focus.
- `2-name`: Your markets
- `2-title`: Where do you sell?
- `2-intro`: Choose your current markets. You can add more as your business grows.
- `3-name`: Your sources
- `3-title`: Build your source list.
- `3-intro`: Choose the official sources your team wants to review. These are watchlist preferences; live monitoring is not active.
- `4-name`: Your briefing
- `4-title`: Make the briefing yours.
- `4-intro`: Set your preferred review rhythm. Email delivery is not active in the public preview.
- `5-name`: Ready to review
- `5-title`: Your watchlist is ready.
- `5-intro`: Review your choices, then explore the workspace with clearly labeled sample updates.

## `fields`

- `business`: Business name
- `business-placeholder`: Your beverage business
- `categories`: Beverage categories
- `markets`: Markets
- `sources`: Sources for your selected markets
- `frequency`: Preferred briefing frequency
- `daily`: Daily
- `weekly`: Weekly
- `briefing-note`: You can export a sample briefing from your workspace. This preference is saved for future setup; it does not schedule a run or an email.

## `summary`

- `business`: Business
- `beverages`: Beverages
- `markets`: Markets
- `sources`: Sources
- `briefing`: Briefing preference
- `free-note`: Preview access is free. No card, account, live monitoring, or paid subscription is created.

## `errors`

- `business`: Add your business name and choose at least one beverage category.
- `markets`: Choose at least one market to continue.
- `sources`: Choose at least one source for your watchlist.
- `storage`: Your browser could not save the workspace. Allow site storage, then try again.
- `reload-needed`: Reload the workspace before saving again.
- `reload`: Reload workspace

## `actions`

- `back`: Back
- `back-to-site`: Back to website
- `continue`: Continue
- `open`: Open my workspace
- `footer`: For human review. Not legal advice.
- `your-data`: Your data
