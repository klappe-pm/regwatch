# changes

The Changes page of the local application: candidate impacts with their reasons, source changes, and unconfirmed candidates from ingest.

## `header`

- `title`: Changes
- `description`: Candidate impacts ranked by focus area, severity and date, then source changes and unconfirmed candidates from ingest.

## `filters`

- `filter`: Filter
- `filter-placeholder`: Obligation, product or reason
- `severity`: Severity
- `all-severities`: All severities
- `focus`: Focus
- `all-changes`: All changes
- `in-focus`: In my focus areas
- `jurisdiction`: Jurisdiction
- `all-jurisdictions`: All jurisdictions

## `impacts`

- `title`: Candidate Impacts ({count})
- `empty`: No candidate impact matches these filters.
- `table`: Candidate impacts
- `severity`: Severity
- `obligation`: Obligation
- `product`: Product
- `effective`: Effective
- `outside-focus`: · outside your focus areas
- `selected`: Selected change

## `detail`

- `prompt`: Select a change to see why it was flagged.
- `product`: Product
- `jurisdiction`: Jurisdiction
- `status`: Status
- `effective`: Effective
- `focus`: Focus
- `all-areas`: All areas
- `outside`: Outside your focus areas
- `obligation`: Obligation
- `why`: Why It Was Flagged

## `sources`

- `title`: Source Changes
- `empty`: Every source matches its accepted baseline.
- `table`: Source changes
- `source`: Source
- `status`: Status
- `detail`: Detail

## `candidates`

- `title`: Unconfirmed Candidates
- `empty`: Documents from the Federal Register style feed that may affect your register will appear here for review.
- `table`: Unconfirmed candidates
- `date`: Date
- `document`: Document
- `agency`: Agency
