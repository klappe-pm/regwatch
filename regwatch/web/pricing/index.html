A Few Useful Details.

What can I use today?

You can set up a portfolio, choose markets and sources, explore synthetic regulatory updates, save review decisions, and export a sample briefing. Your workspace stays in this browser.

Does this preview monitor official sources?

No. The public website demonstrates the review workflow with clearly labeled sample updates. The Regwatch engine supports source checking locally. Hosted monitoring needs a production backend before launch.

Will I be charged automatically?

No. The preview has no payment collection, recurring billing, or paid entitlements. Future subscriptions will require an explicit checkout.

Does Bev Watch give legal advice?

No. Regulatory information and potential impacts require verification against primary sources and review by a qualified person.
