Your Preview Workspace.

What This Website Stores

The public preview stores your company name, product descriptions, selected markets and sources, and review preferences in your browser’s local storage. This is a browser-local workspace, not an account. Avoid entering confidential business information in the preview.

Control Your Data

Export a copy in Workspace settings. Use “Clear this browser workspace” in the same screen to remove the saved workspace, or clear this site’s browser storage. Other browsers and devices do not share this workspace.

Hosting and External Links

The hosting provider may process standard request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to serve the website. Following an official source link opens a separate website with its own privacy practices.

Subscriptions and Email

The preview collects no payment details or email addresses, sends no email briefings, and creates no recurring subscription. A production account service and its privacy terms must be introduced before those features launch.
